Featured application / Financial systems01

MBS Event Processor

Modernizing mortgage-backed securities event processing to handle millions of events a day. Designed to address dropped and duplicate events while keeping downstream loan and payment updates consistent.

JavaSpring BootEvent-driven architectureIdempotencyVersioning

VALIDATE · ORDER · DELIVER

Explore the engineering

The challenge: Dropped and duplicate events could leave downstream loan and payment updates missing or inconsistent.

My contribution: Led design and downstream coordination for the V1-to-V2 modernization, implementing core processing logic as part of a four-engineer team.

The approach: Idempotency and deduplication to handle repeat delivery; partitioning and monotonic versioning to preserve ordering; retries, backpressure, and monitoring to make failures recoverable and visible.

The focus: Reliable processing at millions of events per day, with consistent downstream updates.
